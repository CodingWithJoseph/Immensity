# Immensity
## Opportunity Intelligence for Builders

---

## What It Is

Immensity is a platform for people who want to build companies but aren't sure what to build — or who keep building things nobody wants. It scrapes and analyzes signals from across the web to surface real business opportunities hiding in how people talk online: the complaints, the workarounds, the "does anyone know a tool that does X" posts, the "I'd pay for this" comments.

The goal is to replace guesswork with evidence. Instead of picking an idea based on intuition and hoping the market exists, Immensity shows you where pain is concentrated, how intense it is, and whether people are actively looking for a solution.

---

## The Problem It Solves

Most people who want to build a company get stuck at the same place: they don't know what to build, or they build something and find out too late that nobody cares. The standard advice is to "talk to customers" — but that assumes you already know which problem to investigate.

Immensity starts earlier. It listens to where people already talk about their problems, at scale, without being asked — and turns that into a continuously updated map of opportunities worth investigating.

---

## Who It's For

People who want to build a company and need the market to help them figure out what that should be. That includes first-time builders with no idea yet, experienced founders who've wasted time on the wrong thing before, and anyone who wants their next move to be grounded in real signal rather than a hunch.

---

## What It Does Today

### Trends
The home dashboard surfaces what's gaining momentum across ingested sources — problems that are growing in volume and intensity over time. Not a static snapshot, but a living feed of what's getting louder.

### Search
Keyword search with basic filtering across ingested posts, so you can investigate a specific problem space and see the raw signal behind it.

### Signal Scoring
Each post is scored for willingness to pay, intensity, and intent — the signals that indicate whether a problem is just an annoyance or something people will actually spend money to solve.

### Clustering
Groups of posts describing the same underlying problem, often in completely different words, are surfaced as clusters. A single post is anecdote. A cluster of hundreds of posts across weeks is a pattern worth paying attention to.

### Pipeline & Launch Dashboards
A structured pipeline that moves an opportunity from discovery through to launch, with dedicated views for tracking progress at each stage.

### Concept
A workspace for capturing and developing a specific opportunity — notes, signals, hypotheses, and open questions in one place.

---

## The Direction

The current version is the foundation. Search, signal, and clustering are working but will get significantly stronger. The longer-term vision is for Immensity to feel less like a tool you operate and more like an investigative journalist working on your behalf — continuously monitoring sources, surfacing patterns, and filing reports on opportunities before you know to look for them.

The sources will expand well beyond Reddit. The signal scoring will get more precise. And eventually, agentic AI will automate the research pipeline end-to-end.

---
## What It's Not

Immensity is not a project management tool, a content aggregator, or a market research report you read once. It's a continuously updated intelligence layer between the founder and the market — built to surface the right question before the wrong answer gets built.
